# Vehicle Rental Management System

## Project Statement
The **Vehicle Rental Management System** is designed to streamline the process of renting vehicles by providing a simple, efficient, and user-friendly interface for both customers and administrators. The system ensures smooth operations by tracking vehicle availability, rental transactions, customer details, and billing.

## Objectives
- To maintain a structured database of vehicles, including type, brand, rate, and availability.
- To allow customers to browse available vehicles and rent them seamlessly.
- To enable administrators to manage vehicle inventory, update details, and track rentals.
- To automatically calculate rental charges based on vehicle rates and rental duration.
- To reduce manual paperwork and improve reliability and accuracy.

## Features
- **Vehicle Listing:** View all vehicles with details like ID, type, brand, rate, and availability.
- **Vehicle Rental:** Rent an available vehicle and update its rental status.
- **Return Process:** Mark a rented vehicle as returned and compute total billing.
- **Search Functionality:** Search vehicles by type or brand.
- **Admin Controls:** Add, remove, or update vehicle details.

## Target Users
- **Rental Agencies** looking for an automated and efficient way to manage their fleet.
- **Customers** who want a convenient method to browse and rent vehicle.

## Expected Outcome
The system will simplify the vehicle rental workflow, minimize human errors, and ensure faster, more reliable service delivery. It will serve as a foundational project for learning programming concepts while offering practical real-world utility.
